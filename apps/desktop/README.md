# Tetris Trainer Desktop

The Tetris Trainer desktop app: a frameless window with its own title bar, a play area showing the board, hold, next queue, live stats and the keys being pressed, a panel beside it that lists the lessons or holds the settings, a game-over screen with restart, and lesson screens that walk through rules on annotated boards, demonstrate dealt orders with narrated decisions, and coach practice attempts by marking mistakes on the board.
